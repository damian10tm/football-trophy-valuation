# Football Trophy Valuation

## Project objective

Explore how the difficulty and value of winning football trophies can be evaluated using historical data, statistical methods, and transparent assumptions.

The goal is not to produce one objectively correct ranking, but to investigate how different factors influence our conclusions.

## Initial research question

How concentrated are the winners of major football competitions, and what can this tell us about competitive balance?

## Initial competitions

- FIFA World Cup
- UEFA Champions League
- Premier League
- La Liga

## Initial metrics

- Number of unique winners
- Share of titles won by the three most successful teams
- Frequency of repeat champions
- Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI)

## Future research directions

- Difficulty of winning an individual edition
- Historical team strength and competitive balance
- Qualification difficulty
- Opportunities to compete during a player's career
- Comparing international and club competitions
- Exploring alternative trophy valuation models

## Methodology notes

This is an exploratory learning project. Metrics and methods will evolve as we investigate the data and identify limitations.
